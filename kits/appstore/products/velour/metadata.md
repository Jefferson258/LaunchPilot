## Description

Velour Closet is a fashion-forward digital closet for iPad. Photograph your wardrobe, organize by category and color, and plan outfits with weather-aware suggestions — without creating an account.

**Your closet**  
Add items from camera or photo library. On-device Vision suggests categories; you stay in control of every tag and photo.

**Today**  
See outfit recommendations, similarity hints, and weather context for what to wear.

**Calendar**  
Review logged outfits by day and build a visual history of what you actually wore.

**Privacy by design**  
Wardrobe data is stored locally on your iPad with SwiftData. We do not require sign-in for core features in the current version.

Velour Closet is designed for iPad. Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to add or edit items.
